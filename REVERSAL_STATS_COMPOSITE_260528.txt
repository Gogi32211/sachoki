METRICS (pivot 3-3):
  HH%/HL% = next pivot is Higher-High / Higher-Low(pullback), of resolved pivots. CLEAN.
  gainHH  = avg % up to that HH ; ddHL = avg % drawdown to that HL (finite, |.|<200%).
  f5med   = MEDIAN 5d fwd return (robust) ; f5avg = mean 5d clipped |.|<=100% ; win5 = %>0.

DIRECTION KEY (IMPORTANT — empirical, and OPPOSITE the builder's green/amber colours):
  HH%/HL% describe the STRUCTURE of the next Williams pivot, not the near-term return.
  In this data they are INVERSELY related to the 5d return:
    * HIGH HH%  (e.g. R2H overbought tops, HH 71%) goes with NEGATIVE f5med -> the higher-
      high is the LAST push before a fade. HIGH HH% = exhausted / near-term BEARISH.
    * HIGH HL%  (e.g. R2L oversold, HL 63%) goes with flat-to-POSITIVE f5med -> higher-low
      base building. HIGH HL% = constructive / near-term BULLISH.
  => For near-term direction trust f5med / win5. Read HL% as the bullish-base signal and
     HH% as the exhaustion/topping signal. (The builder paints HH green, HL amber — that
     labelling is about swing structure, not about what price does over the next 5-20 days.)

CAVEATS:
  * Builder dropdown 'SP500 + NASDAQ' actually searches ALL universes incl. Russell2K
    (uni='both' omits the filter). Russell2K penny stocks drive the fwd-return outliers.
  * Builder 'Forward returns' uses RAW AVG(fwd_5d) with inf/extremes -> noisy. Trust f5med.
